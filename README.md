## About

I study the genetic basis of economically important traits in freshwater fish, particularly bighead carp (*Hypophthalmichthys nobilis*) and European perch (*Perca fluviatilis*). My research combines whole-genome resequencing, population genomics, genome-wide association studies, QTL mapping, and transcriptomics to investigate growth, body shape, sex determination, and environmental adaptation.

My work aims to identify genetic variants and candidate genes that can support genetic improvement and molecular breeding in freshwater aquaculture.

## Research Interests

- **Quantitative genetics and trait mapping:** genetic architecture of growth and body shape; heritability estimation, GWAS, and QTL mapping.
- **Fish genomics and population genetics:** genome assembly, genetic variation, population structure, and genomic relatedness.
- **Sex determination:** sex-associated genomic regions and molecular markers.
- **Environmental adaptation:** physiological and transcriptomic responses to temperature, alkalinity, and other environmental challenges.
